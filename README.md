# 🚀 AI Skills vs Job Risk – Global Impact Dashboard (Power BI)

This project presents an interactive **Power BI dashboard** that analyzes the global impact of Artificial Intelligence on jobs, industries, salaries, and skill demand.

It transforms complex datasets into clear, actionable insights to understand how AI is reshaping the future of work.

---

## 📊 Project Overview

The dashboard explores:
- Job Risk vs Job Creation trends  
- AI adoption across industries  
- Salary distribution and growth  
- AI tools performance and impact  
- Skill demand and future opportunities  

---

## ✨ Key Features

- 💱 **Dynamic Currency Conversion**
  - Convert salary values based on selected currency  

- ⚖️ **Job Risk vs Job Created Toggle**
  - Compare impact dynamically across job categories  

- 🔄 **Industry vs Technology Toggle**
  - Switch between industry-level and tool-level analysis  

- 📊 **Top N Analysis & Dense Ranking**
  - Identify top industries by AI investment  

- 🤖 **AI Tools Comparison**
  - Top 5 tools with custom sorting and scoring  

- 📈 **Salary Insights**
  - Pay gap, growth roles vs at-risk roles  

- 🧠 **Skill Insights**
  - Skill demand, salary premium, and growth trends  

- 📚 **AI Learning Hub**
  - Curated AI courses and learning resources  

- 🔍 **Advanced Insights**
  - Trend analysis, forecasting, and workforce insights
